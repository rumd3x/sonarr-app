PRIVACY POLICY - Sonarr (unofficial) for Android
Package: com.rumd3x.sonarr
Last updated: 2026-11-13

1. ABOUT THIS APP

Sonarr (unofficial) is a mobile client that lets you manage your own
Sonarr server from your Android phone. It is an independent project and is
not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the Sonarr project.

2. SUMMARY

We do not collect, store, sell, share or transmit your personal data to
us or to any third party. The app has no accounts, no login, no backend
of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

3. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The following is kept only in the app's private storage on your device
and never leaves it, except as described in section 4:

- The primary URL, the optional secondary URL and the API key of your
  Sonarr server, which you enter yourself.
- A small cache of your series list, used to show the library faster.
- Cached poster and banner images downloaded from your server.
- A marker indicating that demo mode is active, when you use it.

You can erase all of this at any time with "Disconnect & clear data" in
the More screen, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app
from Android settings.

4. NETWORK COMMUNICATION

The app connects only to the server addresses you configure. Requests
(including your API key, sent in the X-Api-Key header or the apikey query
parameter for images) go directly from your device to your own Sonarr
server. We never receive them. Your Sonarr server may in turn contact
other services (indexers, download clients, metadata providers, image
hosts); this is controlled by your server and not by this app. Images
may be loaded from the addresses your server provides.

The app permits unencrypted HTTP connections because Sonarr servers are
often reached over a local network. If you use HTTP outside a trusted
network, your data may be visible to others on that network. We recommend
HTTPS whenever possible.

5. DEMO MODE

Demo mode uses built-in sample data. In demo mode no data is sent to any
server and no network connection to a Sonarr server is made.

6. PERMISSIONS

The app requests only the INTERNET permission, needed to reach your
Sonarr server. It does not access your contacts, location, camera,
microphone, photos, files or any other personal data.

7. THIRD PARTIES

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking
services, and shares no data with third parties.

8. CHILDREN

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children under 13.

9. SECURITY

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which is protected by the
Android operating system. You are responsible for protecting your device
and your Sonarr server and API key.

10. YOUR RIGHTS

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access,
correct or delete. You control all data stored by the app on your device
and can remove it at any time as described in section 3.

11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The latest version is
kept in the app's source repository, and the "Last updated" date above
will be changed accordingly.

12. CONTACT

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project's issue
tracker at https://github.com/rumd3x/sonarr-app/issues
