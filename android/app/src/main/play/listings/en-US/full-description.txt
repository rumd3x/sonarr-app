Sonarr is an unofficial companion app for people who run their own Sonarr server. Browse your TV library, search for episodes, follow downloads and fix what is missing, all from a clean, modern mobile interface.

NO ACCOUNT. NO DATABASE.
There is no sign-up and no login. On first launch you enter the address of your server and your Sonarr API key. That's it. Your settings stay on your device.

RESILIENT CONNECTION
Set a primary and a secondary URL (for example your home network address and your public domain). If the primary one can't be reached, the app switches to the secondary one automatically.

FEATURES
• Library: poster grid or list, search, filters and per-series progress
• Series details: seasons, episodes, monitoring toggles, automatic search, refresh and delete
• Episode details: file and media info, per-episode history and manual (interactive) search
• Manual search that keeps running in the background, even when indexers take minutes to answer, then lets you grab the release you want
• Add series: look up by name or TVDB id, choose root folder, quality profile and monitoring
• Calendar: upcoming and recent episodes grouped by day
• Activity: live download queue with remove/blocklist, plus history
• Wanted: list missing episodes and search for one or all of them
• Server status: version, health checks and disk space
• Fast: your library is cached locally and opens instantly, then refreshes in the background
• Material 3 design with a dark theme

REQUIREMENTS
You need a running Sonarr instance that your phone can reach (v3 or newer API) and its API key, found under Settings > General > Security in Sonarr.
